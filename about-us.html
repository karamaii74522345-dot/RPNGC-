About the RPNGC Criminal Record Management Portal

A professional team dedicated to secure, reliable national criminal records management

Our Mission

To provide a secure, reliable, and efficient digital platform for managing criminal records across all Papua New Guinea regions, enabling law enforcement to protect citizens and maintain national security.

Our Vision

A fully integrated national criminal record system that serves as the backbone of PNG's law enforcement operations, accessible to authorized personnel across all regions with real-time updates and advanced security measures.

About the Royal Papua New Guinea Constabulary

The Royal Papua New Guinea Constabulary (RPNGC) is the national police force of Papua New Guinea, responsible for maintaining law and order across all regions and provinces. With over 8,000 personnel deployed across 156 police stations nationwide, the RPNGC serves a population of over 9 million.

The Criminal Record Management Portal represents a significant modernization initiative, replacing legacy paper-based systems with a secure digital infrastructure that enables seamless coordination between regional commands and police stations.
