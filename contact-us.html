Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my criminal record?

Authorized law enforcement personnel can access records through the secure login portal. If you believe there is an error in your record, contact our Records Department at records@rpngc.gov.pg.

How long does data backup take?

The system performs automatic daily backups during off-peak hours (midnight - 2 AM). Backups typically complete within 2 hours and are stored across multiple secure data centers.

Is the system available 24/7?

Yes, the Criminal Record Management Portal maintains 99.8% uptime with 24/7 availability. Scheduled maintenance windows are announced in advance and typically occur on the first Sunday of each month.

How secure are the records?

All records are encrypted with military-grade 256-bit AES encryption. Access is controlled through multi-factor authentication and role-based permissions, with complete audit trail logging of all access events.

Can I access records from my mobile device?

Yes, we provide a secure mobile application for iOS and Android devices. Officers can access records in the field with automatic synchronization when connection is restored.

How do I report a system issue?

Contact our IT Support team at support@rpngc.gov.pg or call +675 325 0157. For security-related issues, use security@rpngc.gov.pg immediately.
