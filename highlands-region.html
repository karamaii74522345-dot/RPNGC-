Highlands Region

Criminal Records Management for Eastern Highlands, Simbu, Southern Highlands, Hela, Enga, Western Highlands, and Jiwaka Provinces

Regional Overview

The Highlands Region comprises seven provinces in the mountainous interior of Papua New Guinea. This region faces unique law enforcement challenges due to geographical isolation, diverse terrain, and complex tribal systems. The region is economically vital for agriculture and mining operations.

Provinces in Highlands Region

Eastern Highlands Province

Capital: Goroka

High-altitude region known for coffee production and cultural diversity. Focus on organized crime and inter-tribal disputes.

  • Population: ~620,000
  • Active Cases: 580
  • Police Stations: 22

Simbu Province

Capital: Kundiawa

Central mountain region with significant tribal conflicts and resource management challenges.

  • Population: ~330,000
  • Active Cases: 245
  • Police Stations: 12

Southern Highlands Province

Capital: Mendi

Major agricultural and mining hub with significant law enforcement presence for economic crimes.

  • Population: ~710,000
  • Active Cases: 892
  • Police Stations: 28

Hela Province

Capital: Tari

Newest province with major oil and gas operations, enforcement focused on industrial crimes.

  • Population: ~180,000
  • Active Cases: 156
  • Police Stations: 8

Enga Province

Capital: Wabag

Agricultural region with high population density and frequent clan-related violence.

  • Population: ~450,000
  • Active Cases: 234
  • Police Stations: 11

Western Highlands Province

Capital: Mount Hagen

Second-largest city in PNG, significant commercial activity with organized crime concerns.

  • Population: ~520,000
  • Active Cases: 456
  • Police Stations: 19

Jiwaka Province

Capital: Minj

Newly created province in the western highlands with emerging crime patterns.

  • Population: ~310,000
  • Active Cases: 282
  • Police Stations: 9
