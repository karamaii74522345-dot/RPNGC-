New Guinea Islands Region

Criminal Records Management for East New Britain, West New Britain, New Ireland, Bougainville, and Manus Provinces

Regional Overview

The New Guinea Islands Region comprises five provinces in the eastern and northern islands of Papua New Guinea. This region has unique maritime challenges with geographically dispersed communities, international maritime boundaries, and significant fisheries resources requiring specialized law enforcement operations.

Provinces in Islands Region

East New Britain Province

Capital: Rabaul

Island province with significant commercial activity in cocoa and copra production. Major international port with tourism importance.

  • Population: ~380,000
  • Active Cases: 510
  • Police Stations: 18

West New Britain Province

Capital: Kimbe

Palm oil production hub with significant environmental and industrial crime concerns.

  • Population: ~280,000
  • Active Cases: 340
  • Police Stations: 13

New Ireland Province

Capital: Kavieng

Island province with strategic location and fishing industry importance. Focus on maritime security.

  • Population: ~195,000
  • Active Cases: 280
  • Police Stations: 11

Autonomous Region of Bougainville

Capital: Buka Town

Autonomous region with post-conflict recovery focus. Significant mineral resources and international relations importance.

  • Population: ~290,000
  • Active Cases: 490
  • Police Stations: 16

Manus Province

Capital: Lorengau

Remote island province with important fisheries and strategic maritime location near Pacific shipping lanes.

  • Population: ~70,000
  • Active Cases: 72
  • Police Stations: 9
