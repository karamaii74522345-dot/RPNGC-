Momase Region

Criminal Records Management for Madang, Morobe, East Sepik, and West Sepik Provinces

Regional Overview

The Momase Region comprises four provinces on the northern coast of Papua New Guinea. This region is vital for international trade and maritime operations. Provinces include Madang (commercially important), Morobe (contains Lae, the major industrial center), and the two Sepik provinces in the northeast.

Provinces in Momase Region

Madang Province

Capital: Madang

Coastal province with significant fishing and tourism industries. Major international port operations require specialized maritime law enforcement.

  • Population: ~440,000
  • Active Cases: 680
  • Police Stations: 26

Morobe Province

Capital: Lae

Most industrialized province in PNG. Home to Lae, the second-largest city, with major port operations and manufacturing.

  • Population: ~680,000
  • Active Cases: 1,250
  • Police Stations: 42

East Sepik Province

Capital: Wewak

Northern region with significant international trade activities and maritime boundary operations.

  • Population: ~450,000
  • Active Cases: 520
  • Police Stations: 21

West Sepik Province

Capital: Vanimo

Northern border with Indonesia, critical for smuggling prevention and international border management.

  • Population: ~280,000
  • Active Cases: 490
  • Police Stations: 18
