Southern Region

Criminal Records Management for National Capital District, Central, Gulf, Milne Bay, Oro, and Western Provinces

Regional Overview

The Southern Region encompasses six provinces covering critical law enforcement areas. This region includes the National Capital District (Port Moresby), the political and administrative heart of Papua New Guinea, along with five other strategically important provinces.

Provinces in Southern Region

National Capital District

Capital: Port Moresby

The administrative center of PNG. Contains the highest concentration of urban crime cases and serves as the primary coordination hub for the entire nation.

  • Population: ~380,000
  • Active Cases: 3,200
  • Police Stations: 42

Central Province

Capital: Dio

Located in the southeastern region, known for maritime security and fishing-related enforcement operations.

  • Population: ~260,000
  • Active Cases: 1,450
  • Police Stations: 28

Gulf Province

Capital: Kerema

Borders Indonesia, critical for border security and trafficking prevention operations.

  • Population: ~180,000
  • Active Cases: 890
  • Police Stations: 18

Milne Bay Province

Capital: Alotau

Eastern PNG maritime region, focus on coastal security and international border management.

  • Population: ~270,000
  • Active Cases: 1,320
  • Police Stations: 32

Oro Province

Capital: Popondetta

Borders Solomon Islands with significant maritime law enforcement responsibilities.

  • Population: ~190,000
  • Active Cases: 980
  • Police Stations: 21

Western Province

Capital: Daru

Western border with Indonesia, primary focus on trafficking prevention and border security.

  • Population: ~210,000
  • Active Cases: 1,102
  • Police Stations: 15
